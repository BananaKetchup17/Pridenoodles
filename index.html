The rosy wok recipe

Sweet beet, savory soy, and a little sesame make a glossy pink sauce that clings to every noodle.

⏱ 25 minutes · Serves 2

Gather your ingredients

  • 200 g noodles
  • 1 small cooked beet
  • 1 carrot, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 egg (optional)

Let's make it

  1. Wash your hands and produce. Cook noodles according to the packet; drain and set aside. Blend the cooked beet with soy sauce, sesame oil, and a splash of water until smooth.
  2. Warm a little oil in a clean wok or pan. Stir-fry carrot and cabbage until tender-crisp, about 3–4 minutes. Add the sauce and heat until bubbling gently.
  3. Add noodles and toss until hot throughout and evenly coated. If adding egg, scramble it in the pan and cook until fully set. Finish with spring onion and sesame seeds, then serve right away.
Little kitchen-safety note Use clean utensils and keep raw ingredients separate from cooked food. Cook egg until fully set, reheat leftovers until steaming hot, and refrigerate them within 2 hours.
